Deadline Helper

Our shared class planner. This page shows every deadline we have, and Deadline Bot on Telegram handles the reminding.

  1. Add a deadline on the yellow note below, or text it to Deadline Bot on Telegram. A sentence, a photo of the syllabus or a PDF all work.
  2. It lands in one shared list. Duplicates are skipped and finished deadlines disappear on their own, so the board stays tidy for everyone.
  3. Get reminded on Telegram. First open Deadline Bot and send /start to switch it on. Then pick a time and how often under “Want reminders?”, or send /reminders to the bot. In the bot, /deadlines shows the list and “delete the …” removes one.
